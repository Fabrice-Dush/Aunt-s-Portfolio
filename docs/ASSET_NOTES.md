# Asset provenance and publication policy

- `design/prototype/assets/seraphine-standing.webp` (1122×1402, compressed WebP) and `seraphine-standing-small.webp` (780×975) are **AI-generated design portraits** based on the user-provided seated photograph.
- The original seated photograph is not packaged in this project folder. The 5'10"–5'11" height instruction influences the composition but cannot verify her actual physical proportions from a seated image.
- The depicted treatment facility is **illustrative and generated**. Do not imply this was photographed at WASAC or is a real company property.
- Portrait requires Seraphine's approval for accuracy and publication; if not approved use an authentic portrait or another approved illustration.
- `design/prototype/assets/icons.svg` contains custom local SVG stroke icons for the visual design concepts.
- No third-party font binaries or external image hotlinks are included. System font stacks are used.
- All other content imagery or logos added in later phases must have appropriate owner permission or licenses and truthful context.
